About Me

Hello and welcome to my website!

I am quant who is passionate about mathematics and software engineering. I especially enjoy working at their intersection: building elegant software that brings complex mathematical models and algorithms to life.

My two favourite programming languages are Rust and Python. I like Python because it is expressive, quick to write, and versatile. Its scientific stack makes it a great choice for mathematical research and data analysis.

When performance matters, I enjoy going low-level. I am a Rust enthusiast because it gives me a lot of control and performance without having to give up memory safety. Also, it brings with it a very convenient toolkit.

I enjoy Mathematical Finance because it brings beautiful and abstract mathematics from probability, measure theory and stochastic calculus into the real world.

I hold a PhD in Applied Mathematics; the focus of my thesis was rough volatility modelling, a family of stochastic volatility models.

Currently, I work in Counterparty Credit Risk as a Senior Risk Quant.
